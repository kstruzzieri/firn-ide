The rules, exit criteria and release steps are in [README.md](README.md).
The milestone mirrors In and Stretch; the v0.16.0 items expected to roll stay
in that milestone until its freeze on 2026-11-24.

**Theme:** model discovery. A provider lists the models its current key can
use, every capability says where its answer came from, a model can be checked
before Golem uses it, and a run a provider refuses for a reason other than
credentials or a rate limit names the provider instead of reading "The Golem
run failed." This is the second slice of the Golem model discovery design as
the issues #490 to #503 record it, the slice that cites go-llm v0.8.0 APIs.
go-llm v0.8.0 was tagged on 2026-11-20 with go-llm#734 to go-llm#737 and
go-llm#740; go-llm#738, go-llm#739 and go-llm#741 moved to go-llm v0.9.0.

Beside the theme, the ground under Firn moves: its host git calls refuse a
planted bare repository, the terminal and everything else Firn starts get the
user's login environment, Wails leaves its beta, and CI leaves the
`ubuntu-22.04` image before GitHub retires it.

## In

Sized in days (S 0.5, M 1.5, L 4, XL 8): 24.0 against about 8 working days.
The calendar gives fourteen working days from Wed 2026-11-25, the day after
v0.16.0's freeze, to Mon 2026-12-14, the day before this one. Thanksgiving
(Thu 2026-11-26) takes one, v0.16.0's release work (its soak checks, the
release on Tue 2026-12-01 and the back-merge) about two, and the
release-candidate pass and its fix days under Schedule three. In runs over by
owner decision. The estimates overstate the elapsed time: v0.16.0's In, 19.5
days against 9 to 10 working days, merged from 2026-11-07 to 2026-11-09 (PRs
#530 to #535, #540 and #541), and that train's time went to its
release-candidate pass and the fix it drove (#602). That pace may not carry
over: v0.16.0's In issues had every decision made before code started, and
here #562, #527 and #605 each make theirs in their pull request. So the
schedule protects the pass, and if In does not fit, the dates move.

### Schedule

- **Tue 2026-11-24:** `release/v0.16.0` is cut. #480, #526 and #527 lead on
  `develop`.
- **Tue 2026-12-01:** v0.16.0 releases, and its back-merge follows.
- **Wed 2026-12-09, Stretch stops.** Stretch work starts only once In has
  merged. This departs from the README, which lets Stretch merge up to the
  freeze, so that the release-candidate pass covers everything that ships.
- **Thu 2026-12-10, release-candidate pass** on a packaged build of
  `develop`, with Fri 2026-12-11 and Mon 2026-12-14 held for its fixes.
- **Fri 2026-12-11:** v0.18.0's contract is drafted.
- **If In is late, the dates move.** When In has not merged in time for the
  release-candidate pass, the pass, the Stretch stop, the freeze and the
  release move together by amendment, decided as late as the work allows. The
  release stays at least seven days after the freeze and waits until all exit
  criteria pass.

## Stretch

Ships only if it merges to `develop` by the stop date under Schedule.

Work starts in this order once In has merged:

1. #562, piece 3: a byte-safe output stream with a beginning and an end. A
   multi-byte character split across two PTY reads reaches xterm.js as
   replacement characters, so long non-ASCII output is corrupted (C1). A
   shell that exits by itself leaves a frozen tab and an unreaped process
   (D2). The piece also gives the PTY its size before the shell prints, wires
   binary input, reports failed writes and orders a resize against a close
   (C2 to C4, D3).
2. #429: a keyboard user cannot leave a terminal (WCAG 2.1.2), the terminal
   input does not name its session, and tabbing out of the session menu can
   drop focus to the page. Rolls from v0.16.0.
3. #606: undock the terminal into its own window and dock it back, keeping
   its shells and history. Its four open questions (what undocks, where it
   can dock, where history comes from, and whether Output and Problems go
   with it) are answered in the issue before code starts. After #526 and
   #562's pieces 1 to 3.

Unranked, after those: #529 (set the context window on a model already in the
file, after #496, reusing a limit the provider advertises if discovery offers
one), #353 (choose a route's role in the editor, which #498 waits on), #461
(Go to File) and #463 (rename in place).

Expected to roll from v0.16.0's Stretch list at its freeze on 2026-11-24, as
the README provides: #463, #461, #452, #445, #444, #443, #436, #434, #433,

## Exit criteria

The criteria in [README.md](README.md), plus:

- A VoiceOver pass over Refresh models and its listing announcements (#495),
  over Check and use, its prompt, its outcome announcements and Details
  (#496), and over Try another model and its return to Retry (#497).
- The smoke pass runs the packaged app opened from Finder: a terminal and a
  run profile find a tool Homebrew installed, and the terminal echoes
  non-ASCII input unchanged (#562).
- The final smoke pass adds #526's window checks: undock Golem and close its
  window, use a file dialog, and quit while a run profile is running.
- No `GLIBC_` version that `objdump -T` lists for the Linux binary is newer
  than glibc 2.35, Ubuntu 22.04's, or than the floor #527 records in the
  README if it raises it.
